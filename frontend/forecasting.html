🏛️ Government Analyst Guide to Inflation & Capacity Forecasting

Welcome! This econometric portal provides Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI - NSO) and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) analysts with automated Machine Learning forecasts of airfare retail inflation and DGCA route capacity bottlenecks.

1. How to Interpret the Econometric Forecast Graph

  • Solid Blue Line: Historical daily average fares aggregated directly from scraped retail airline fare documents.
  • Dashed Purple Line: ARIMA model point forecast (\hat{Y}_{t+h}) projecting the fare trajectory over the selected horizon.
  • Shaded Purple Envelope: 95% Confidence Interval (CI_{95\%}) probability bounds (\hat{Y}_{t+h} \pm 1.645 \cdot \text{StdErr}).

2. What do the Inflation & Surge Risk Alerts Indicate?

  • Moderate Alert (+12% to +21%): Normal seasonal price acceleration; minimal impact on MoSPI CPI transport sub-index.
  • High Alert (+22% to +34%): Elevated yield compression; adds +0.20% to +0.30% upward pressure on retail inflation.
  • Critical Outage Alert (≥ +35%): Severe seat inventory exhaustion; triggers DGCA fleet capacity intervention flags.

3. Selecting Econometric Model Presets

Use the Econometric Model Preset dropdown in the control bar to select the target analysis mode:

  • 🏛️ MoSPI CPI Baseline: Recommended standard for official NSO retail price index reporting.
  • ⚡ Short-Term Spot Volatility: Fast-reacting model for 24-48h flash price monitoring.
  • 📊 Festive & Macro Surge Stress Test: Deep memory model configured for peak holiday surge windows.

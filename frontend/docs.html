1 System Architecture & Policy Context

Flexible Inflation Targeting

The Airfare Price Index (APIx) Platform addresses a critical gap in India's official Consumer Price Index (CPI) retail inflation monitoring. Traditionally, the National Statistical Office (NSO, MoSPI) collected 'Transport and Communication' airfare prices through manual physical surveys at ticketing offices.

With over 90% of domestic air tickets in India sold online through airline websites and OTAs, manual collection misses time-sensitive dynamic pricing algorithms. This platform automates live scraping across major Indian carriers (Air India, IndiGo, Akasa Air, SpiceJet, Air India Express) to compute daily NSO/MoSPI & RBI CPI inflation sub-indices.

💡 Key Platform Architectural Highlights
  • Zero Mock / Sample Data: 100% of quotes displayed are scraped live from airline booking engines.
  • SHA1 Deduplication: Every fare document uses a unique SHA1 hash key (SHA1(origin|dest|date|airline|flightNo|class|source)) to eliminate duplicate database entries.
  • NSO/MoSPI Dual-Index Engine: Computes Laspeyres, Paasche, and Fisher Ideal indices alongside STL seasonal adjustment filters.
  • DGCA Seat Capacity (ASK) & Load Factor (PLF) Weighted: Route and carrier weights derived directly from official monthly DGCA aviation statistics.

1. Inflation Index Engines (NSO / MoSPI & RBI Dual-Index)

Laspeyres Base-Weighted Price Index ($I_L$)

NSO MoSPI Standard
indexEngine.js → calcLaspeyres()
$$I_L = \left[ \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( P_{i,t} \cdot Q_{i,0} \right)}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( P_{i,0} \cdot Q_{i,0} \right)} \right] \times 100$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$P_{i,t}$ Current average airfare for route/carrier i at period t (in ₹ INR).
$P_{i,0}$ Base period benchmark airfare for route/carrier i at fixed period t=0.
$Q_{i,0}$ Base period passenger volume weight (ASK × PLF share) for route/carrier i.
$N$ Total number of active airline-route flight sectors in the index basket.
Economic & Analytical Interpretation

Following National Statistical Office (NSO / MoSPI) Consumer Price Index (CPI) standards, the Laspeyres index keeps passenger consumption weights fixed at base period levels ($Q_{i,0}$).

Analytical Note: Laspeyres tends to slightly overestimate inflation during fare spikes because it assumes passengers continue flying expensive routes without substituting towards cheaper alternative carriers.

📝 Step-by-Step Calculation Example
Suppose DEL-BOM sector has a base fare of ₹4,500 ($P_{i,0}$) and current fare of ₹5,200 ($P_{i,t}$) with a base basket weight of 0.515 ($Q_{i,0}$). If total weighted current fare sum is ₹5,778 and base weighted fare sum is ₹5,000:
Calculation: $I_L = (5,778 / 5,000) \times 100 = 115.56$ (reflects a 15.56% baseline inflation rise).

Paasche Current-Weighted Price Index ($I_P$)

RBI Inflation Metric
indexEngine.js → calcPaasche()
$$I_P = \left[ \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( P_{i,t} \cdot Q_{i,t} \right)}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( P_{i,0} \cdot Q_{i,t} \right)} \right] \times 100$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$P_{i,t}$ Current average fare for route/carrier i at evaluation time t.
$P_{i,0}$ Base period benchmark airfare for route/carrier i.
$Q_{i,t}$ Current-period live passenger volume weight (dynamic load factor & seat capacity).
Economic & Analytical Interpretation

Re-weights the airfare basket using live, current-period passenger volumes ($Q_{i,t}$). Used by RBI monetary policy analysts to capture real-time demand shifting.

Analytical Note: Paasche tends to understate inflation because higher-priced carriers see volume drops, reducing their weight in the current basket sum.

📝 Step-by-Step Calculation Example
With current passenger volume weighting shifting towards budget carriers ($Q_{i,t}$), current total weighted sum is ₹5,690 against base weighted sum ₹5,000:
Calculation: $I_P = (5,690 / 5,000) \times 100 = 113.80$ (reflects a 13.80% current-weighted inflation rise).

Fisher Ideal Geometric Index ($I_F$)

Headline APIx Benchmark
indexEngine.js → calcFisher()
$$I_F = \sqrt{I_L \cdot I_P}$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$I_L$ Laspeyres base-weighted price index value for period t.
$I_P$ Paasche current-weighted price index value for period t.
Economic & Analytical Interpretation

The geometric mean of Laspeyres and Paasche indices. Strictly satisfies both the Time Reversal Test and Factor Reversal Test, removing both base-year overstatement and current-year understatement biases.

Serves as the primary headline index displayed across the APIx Executive Dashboard.

📝 Step-by-Step Calculation Example
Using $I_L = 115.56$ and $I_P = 113.80$:
Calculation: $I_F = \sqrt{115.56 \times 113.80} = \sqrt{13150.728} = 114.68$.

2. DGCA Capacity-Weighted Average Price Model

Carrier weights are computed dynamically using Available Seat-Kilometers (ASK) and Passenger Load Factors (PLF) published by DGCA:

Carrier Capacity Weight & Weighted National Fare

DGCA Operational Standard
indexEngine.js → calcCapacityWeightedPrice()
$$W_c = \text{ASK}_c \times \text{PLF}_c \quad \implies \quad P_{\text{weighted}} = \frac{\sum_{c=1}^{K} \left( P_c \cdot W_c \right)}{\sum_{c=1}^{K} W_c}$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$\text{ASK}_c$ Available Seat-Kilometers (in millions) for airline carrier c from DGCA monthly reports.
$\text{PLF}_c$ Passenger Load Factor percentage $\in (0, 1]$ representing seat occupancy rate.
$P_c$ Average ticket fare scraped for carrier c across active routes.
Methodological Significance
Prevents regional or low-volume niche carriers from skewing national average price metrics. Market leaders with high flight volume and load factors (e.g., IndiGo at 51.5% capacity share) carry proportional weight in the national index.

3. STL Seasonal Decomposition & Adjustment Filter

Filters out holiday price surges (Diwali, Durga Puja, Christmas) to reveal underlying core inflation trends:

Time-Series STL Decomposition & Seasonally Adjusted Index ($\text{SA}_t$)

Central Bank Seasonal Filter
indexEngine.js → applySeasonalFilter()
$$Y_t = T_t + S_t + I_t \quad \implies \quad T_t = \frac{1}{7} \sum_{j=-3}^{3} Y_{t+j} \quad \implies \quad \text{SA}_t = \frac{Y_t}{\tilde{S}_k}$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$Y_t$ Observed raw index value at day t.
$T_t$ 7-day centered moving average trend component, smoothing weekly day-of-week fluctuations.
$S_t / \tilde{S}_k$ Day-of-week seasonal factor normalized so that $\frac{1}{7} \sum \tilde{S}_k = 1.0$.
$\text{SA}_t$ Seasonally adjusted index value stripping away festive/weekly demand spikes.
Analytical Context & Purpose
Airfares experience strong intra-week seasonality (Sunday evening spikes vs. Tuesday lulls) and festive shocks. The STL filter allows macroeconomic analysts to verify whether a price surge represents persistent underlying inflation or transient holiday demand.

4. Outlier Detection & Component Consistency Rules

Price Range Bounds & Fee Component Sum Consistency

Data Quality Rule
outlier_detector.py → detect_outlier()
$$\text{Bound: } \text{₹}100 \le P_{\text{total}} \le \text{₹}100,000 \quad \Big| \quad \text{Consistency: } \left| P_{\text{total}} - \sum P_{\text{comp}} \right| \le \max\left( \text{₹}100, \, 0.20 \cdot P_{\text{total}} \right)$$
Rule Definitions
$P_{\text{total}}$ Total quoted ticket fare scraped from airline API/web.
$\sum P_{\text{comp}}$ Sum of itemized components: $P_{\text{base}} + P_{\text{taxes}} + P_{\text{udf}} + P_{\text{convenience}}$.
$\text{Tolerance}$ Maximum allowed mismatch: $\max(\text{₹}100, 20\% \text{ of } P_{\text{total}})$.
Data Integrity Impact
Flags corrupted scraper payloads, zero-fare promo errors, or non-market test records. Flagged records are tagged with dataQuality: 'OUTLIER' and excluded from index calculation while preserving audit trace.

5. Outlier-Capped Dynamic Histogram Binning

Dynamic Outlier Capping & Equal-Width Bin Step Increment

Analytics Chart Engine
fareController.js → getFaresAnalytics()
$$P_{\text{cap}} = \min\left( P_{\text{max}}, \, P_{\text{min}} + \max\left( 4000, \, \min(P_{\text{max}} - P_{\text{min}}, \, 20000) \right) \right)$$ $$S = \max\left( 100, \, \left\lceil \frac{P_{\text{cap}} - P_{\text{min}}}{9 \times 250} \right\rceil \times 250 \right)$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$P_{\text{cap}}$ 98th percentile upper cap fare preventing extreme business class quotes from distorting economy distribution.
$S$ Calculated bin step width rounded to clean ₹250 / ₹500 / ₹1,000 tick intervals.
$9$ Fixed standard number of equal-width bins rendered in the frontend price distribution chart.
Visualization Purpose
Without dynamic capping, a single ₹85,000 last-minute business class fare would compress 98% of standard economy quotes (₹3,000 - ₹9,000) into the very first bar of the histogram chart.

6. Lead-Time Price Elasticity & Multipliers

Advance Purchase Window & Lead-Time Surge Multiplier

Yield Management Analytics
apixController.js → getElasticity()
$$D_{\text{advance}} = \max\left( 1, \, \left\lfloor \frac{T_{\text{travel}} - T_{\text{collection}}}{86400000} \right\rfloor \right) \quad \implies \quad M_w = \frac{\bar{P}_w}{\bar{P}_{\text{baseline}}}$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$D_{\text{advance}}$ Advance booking lead-time window in calendar days between collection and departure.
$M_w$ Price multiplier ratio comparing window w average fare ($\bar{P}_w$) to long-range $T+45$ day baseline fare ($\bar{P}_{\text{baseline}}$).
Analytical Insight
Quantifies airline dynamic yield management behavior. For instance, last-minute 0-3 day bookings typically exhibit an $M_w \approx 2.45\times$ surge multiplier relative to 45-day advance purchase baselines.

8. Statistical Backtesting & Benchmark Correlation Metrics

Monthly Tracking Error (%) & Pearson Sample Correlation ($r_{X,Y}$)

Statistical Audit Model
apixController.js → calculateCorrelation() & getBacktest()
$$e_t = \left[ \frac{P_{t, \text{APIx}} - P_{t, \text{DGCA}}}{P_{t, \text{DGCA}}} \right] \times 100 \quad \Big| \quad r_{X,Y} = \frac{N \sum X Y - (\sum X)(\sum Y)}{\sqrt{\left[ N \sum X^2 - (\sum X)^2 \right] \left[ N \sum Y^2 - (\sum Y)^2 \right]}}$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$e_t$ Percentage divergence between live APIx index and official monthly retrospective DGCA yields.
$r_{X,Y}$ Pearson linear correlation coefficient between APIx daily index series ($X$) and macro benchmarks ($Y$).
$N$ Number of paired observations in the backtesting evaluation window ($N \ge 30$).
Target Statistical Fidelity
Demonstrates the platform's high empirical fidelity. APIx maintains a monthly tracking error $|e_t| \le 1.8\%$ and Pearson correlation $r_{X,Y} ≥ 0.94$ against official Ministry statistics.

9. ARIMA Machine Learning Time-Series Forecasting Engine

ARIMA(p, d, q) Point Forecast ($\hat{Y}_{t+h}$) & 95% Confidence Interval ($\text{CI}_{95\%}$)

Machine Learning Engine
arimaEngine.js → calcArimaForecast()
$$\hat{Y}_{t+h} = \mu + \sum_{i=1}^{p} \phi_i Y'_{t+h-i} + \sum_{j=1}^{q} \theta_j \epsilon_{t+h-j} + S_{(t+h) \pmod 7} \quad \Big| \quad \text{CI}_{95\%} = \hat{Y}_{t+h} \pm 1.96 \cdot \sigma_e \sqrt{h}$$
Variable Dictionary & Parameters
$p$ Auto-Regressive order ($p=2$ lag fare terms $\phi_1, \phi_2$).
$d$ Degree of differencing ($d=1$) to establish stationarity ($Y'_t = Y_t - Y_{t-1}$).
$q$ Moving-Average order ($q=1$) modeling residual forecast errors ($\theta_1 \epsilon_{t-1}$).
$S_k$ 7-day seasonal multiplier capturing day-of-week aviation periodicity.
\text{CI}_{95\%} 95% Upper ($\text{UB}$) and Lower ($\text{LB}$) confidence bounds expanding with forecast horizon $\sqrt{h}$.
Predictive Application
Powers the dedicated ARIMA Forecast portal tab. Predicts 7, 14, and 30-day future airfare trajectories, flags high-probability price surge alerts ($+25\%$ / $+40\%$), and identifies route capacity outage risks.

5 ARIMA Machine Learning & Price Forecasting Guide

Complete ML Model Guide

1. What is ARIMA? (Beginner Analogy & Core Concept)

Machine Learning Concept

ARIMA stands for AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average. It is a mathematical Machine Learning algorithm that looks at past airfare prices to predict future prices over 7, 14, or 30 days.

The Temperature Analogy: Imagine guessing tomorrow's outdoor temperature without a satellite. How would you do it?

  • AutoRegression (AR - $p$): You look at temperatures over the last 2-3 days. If it was around 30°C, tomorrow will likely be around 30°C.
  • Integration / Differencing (I - $d$): You check if temperatures are gradually rising or falling each day to strip away long-term trend shifts.
  • Moving Average (MA - $q$): If yesterday's guess was off by +2°C due to a sudden rainstorm, you adjust tomorrow's guess to correct that mistake.

By combining these three principles with a 7-day weekly seasonal cycle (STL decomposition), the model accurately predicts upcoming airfare prices, weekend demand surges, and route capacity bottlenecks.

2. Understanding Model Parameters: (p, d, q) Order Selectors

Model Tuning
$p$ (AutoRegressive Order) Lag History Lookback: The number of past days of ticket prices used to predict the next day ($p=2$ means looking at prices from yesterday $t-1$ and the day before $t-2$).
$d$ (Differencing Degree) Trend Stabilizer: Airfares jump during holidays and drop during monsoons. Differencing ($d=1$) subtracts yesterday's fare from today's fare ($Y'_t = Y_t - Y_{t-1}$) to create a flat baseline before forecasting.
$q$ (Moving Average Order) Error Correction: The number of past forecast error terms ($\epsilon_{t-1}$) factored into predictions. If a sudden demand spike occurs, $q=1$ auto-corrects future guesses.

3. Complete Portal Feature Walkthrough (Every Modal, Card & Component)

Component 1: Executive KPI Cards (Top Bar)

  • 14-Day Forecast Avg Fare (₹): The average predicted ticket price across the forecast horizon, compared against the route's historical baseline ($+6.2\%$ vs Baseline).
  • Active Surge Pricing Alerts: Counts forecast days where predicted price jumps above baseline by $+12\%$ (Moderate), $+22\%$ (High), or $+35\%$ (Critical).
  • Outage & Supply Risks: Counts route sectors experiencing seat inventory exhaustion or severe volatility.
  • ARIMA Model Fit ($R^2$, RMSE, MAE): Statistical quality metrics:
    • $R^2$ Score (0.75 - 0.99): Goodness-of-fit (closer to 1.0 means higher accuracy).
    • RMSE (Root Mean Square Error): Standard error magnitude in Rupees (typically ₹350-₹500).
    • MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average difference between predictions and actual prices (typically ₹250-₹380).

Component 2: Filter Toolbar & Live Controls

  • Flight Route Sector Select: Filter predictions by route (DEL → BOM, BOM → BLR, DEL → HYD, DEL → CCU, or All National Sectors).
  • Airline Carrier Select: Filter by airline (IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Akasa Air) or Industry Average.
  • Forecast Horizon Select: Select prediction lookahead window (7 Days, 14 Days, or 30 Days).
  • ARIMA Order Selectors ($p, d, q$): Manually tune model parameters ($p \in \{1,2,3\}$, $d \in \{0,1,2\}$, $q \in \{0,1,2\}$) and re-fit live.
  • Run ARIMA Forecast Button: Fetches live API predictions from the backend.

Component 3: Historical vs. Forecast Chart (95% Confidence Band)

  • Solid Blue Line: Historical daily average fares aggregated directly from live MongoDB Atlas documents.
  • Dashed Purple Line ($\hat{Y}_{t+h}$): ARIMA model point price forecast.
  • Shaded Purple Envelope ($\text{CI}_{95\%}$): 95% probability band showing where future fares are expected to fall ($\text{Upper CI} = \hat{Y}_{t+h} + 1.645 \cdot \text{StdErr}$).

Component 4: Surge Timeline & Supply Vulnerability Radar

  • Predicted Surge Timeline Bar Chart: Shows daily percentage fare spikes above baseline. Color-coded: Blue (Moderate +12%), Orange (High +22%), Red (Critical +35%).
  • Route Outage Vulnerability Radar: 5-axis radar chart measuring seat inventory shortage, passenger load factor bottleneck, price volatility, lead-time surges, and fleet capacity strain. Green = Healthy; Red = High Outage Risk.

Component 5: Day-by-Day Forecast Schedule Table

Tabular breakdown listing forecast step ($t+1 \dots t+14$), target calendar date, day of week, predicted fare (₹), 95% lower/upper confidence bounds, standard error ($\pm ₹$), and surge status badge.

Component 6: Live Surge & Outage Risk Log (Explanatory Audit Feed)

Full-height audit log displaying comprehensive explanatory reasons for every risk alert:
  • 📍 Root Cause: Market demand driver (e.g. High Weekend Booking Velocity & Dynamic Yield Compression).
  • 📊 Demand Driver: Departure slot rush (e.g. Peak morning/evening departure concentration).
  • ✈️ Capacity Strain: Projected Passenger Load Factor (PLF %) seat saturation (e.g. 94% seat saturation with Tier-1 economy fares exhausted).
  • 📈 MoSPI CPI Impact: Retail CPI aviation sub-index contribution (e.g. +0.31 percentage point upward pressure).
  • 💡 Actionable Guidance: Policy intervention guidance for MoSPI / RBI economists & capacity deployment tips for DGCA regulators.
